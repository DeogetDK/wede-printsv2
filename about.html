About WEDE PRINTS

The story behind Pretoria's favourite print shop

How It Started

From One Heat Press to a Full Print Shop

WEDE PRINTS was founded in 2019 by Wede Nkosi, a Pretoria-based entrepreneur with a passion for design and a frustration with overpriced, slow printing services. Starting with nothing more than a single heat press machine and a laptop in his garage, Wede began printing custom T-shirts for local schools and small businesses.

Word spread quickly. Within a year, demand had outgrown the garage and WEDE PRINTS moved into its first commercial space in Pretoria. The business invested in professional printing equipment and expanded its service offering to include commercial printing, large-format banners, and branded business stationery.

Today, WEDE PRINTS serves hundreds of clients ranging from one-person startups to established companies, all with the same commitment to quality and affordability that started it all.
