Our Story

Built on Ink and Ambition

WEDE PRINTS started in 2019 as a home-based T-shirt printing operation run by Wede Nkosi from Pretoria. What began with a single heat press and a lot of determination has grown into a fully equipped print shop serving hundreds of clients across the city.

We believe great printing should be accessible to everyone — whether you're a small business, a student, or an event organiser on a budget.
