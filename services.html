Our Services

Everything we print, and what it costs

Service 01

Commercial Printing

Our commercial printing service covers all your business marketing materials. Whether you need 100 flyers for a local event or 5,000 brochures for a product launch, we handle short and long print runs with consistent quality every time.

Products include: flyers, brochures, posters, catalogues, booklets, and postcards.

From R150 per 100 units

Service 02

Custom T-Shirt Printing

Our roots are in custom T-shirt printing and it remains one of our most popular services. Send us your design or let our team create something from scratch. We use heat transfer and direct-to-garment (DTG) methods for vibrant, lasting results.

Available on a range of garment types, sizes, and colours.

From R120 per shirt

Service 03

Banners & Signage

Make a big impression with our large-format printing. From outdoor banners and pull-up stands to foam board displays and window graphics, we produce signage that's built to last and impossible to ignore.

Available in various sizes, materials, and finishing options including eyelets and hemming.

From R350 per unit

Service 04

Business Stationery

First impressions count. Our business stationery range includes professionally printed business cards, letterheads, compliment slips, envelopes, and notepads. Available in a variety of paper stocks and finishes.

Matte, gloss, and silk lamination options available on business cards.

Business cards from R200 per 250
